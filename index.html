Our Traditional Process

The Art of Herbal Infusion

Quality starts long before a product reaches your hands. We use a slow, intentional process to preserve the pure qualities of every ingredient.

Step 01

Selecting Premium Herbs

We carefully choose a blend of traditional herbs, including Amla, Reetha, Shikakai, Rosemary Leaves, Black Cumin, Methi Dana, and Ratanjot.

Step 02

Slow, Low-Temperature Infusion

Unlike quick-production methods, our herbs are infused slowly at a controlled low temperature for approximately 8 hours. This allows the oils to absorb natural properties while avoiding excessive heat exposure.

Step 03

Filtration & Purification

After infusion, the herbs are carefully filtered. The pure extracts are separated, allowed to cool naturally, and undergo strict final quality checks before packaging.

Step 04

Premium Cold-Pressed Oils

We blend our formula using a base of premium cold-pressed oils—including Argan, Jojoba, Almond, and Pumpkin Seed. These are valued because they are extracted purely without high heat processing.
