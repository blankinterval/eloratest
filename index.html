How to Use

For Best Results

Part dry hair and apply a generous amount directly onto the scalp and roots. Massage gently with fingertips for 5–10 minutes to encourage circulation, then work remaining oil through the lengths. Leave for at least 2–4 hours or overnight before washing.

Commercial hair oils boil herbs rapidly at high temperatures, scorching the essential vitamins and antioxidants. Our 8-hour low-temperature infusion gently draws natural nutrients out of the roots and seeds into cold-pressed Argan, Jojoba, Almond, and Pumpkin Seed oils.
